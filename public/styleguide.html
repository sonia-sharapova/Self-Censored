01 · Principles

How it should feel

  • privacy4all's frame, Self-Censored's paper. The censor bar runs flat across the very top. Below it: a frosted left sidebar, then one header row with the Averia Libre title and a short line under it on the left, and a small search bar on the right. The longer site description sits at the bottom of the sidebar. No right-hand nav.
  • The sidebar is the control panel, and never scrolls. It is exactly the screen height below the sticky censor bar; favourites that don't fit become "+N more". Topics and Redacted keywords ("× name" lists: the name opens its place in Settings, the × removes it), favourite sources (the same, opening each outlet's page; faded when not in the feed), then Bias and Mood only when switched on, then Reset all filters (red link) and Settings, and the site description last. Refresh is the first item in the summary row above the feed. The feed itself has no tabs.
  • Search finds, settings hide. The search bar only ever narrows to articles that mention a word. Words to avoid live in Settings; what they hide is listed in the collapsible summary bar above the feed.
  • One text width. .header__inner and .content__inner share --content-max, so the title and the feed line up.
  • Ruled, square and flat. Collections sit in hairline grids. Buttons, chips, tags, fields and the pager all use a 2px radius, like the containers, with flat fills and no shadows.
  • One reading voice. Trebuchet for everything you read, including descriptions. Averia Libre only for the title and the sidebar mark; IBM Plex Mono only for the search bar, buttons and pager.
  • Colour means something. Blue = primary action and selected tab. Pink = hidden, avoided or destructive (maroon on hover). Green = only show. Yellow = limited. Red = words you've chosen (darker on hover, click to remove).

04 · Space & shape

4px base

Spacing uses --space-1…--space-8. Radii: --radius-chip and --radius-btn are both 2px, so buttons have the same corners as containers, chips, fields and the pager. The censor bar is square.

05 · Layout

Shell & sidebar

The .censor-bar comes first, before .shell. .shell is a 240px sticky .sidebar plus .shell__main: a .header (one row: .title-block left, .header__search right), the frosted .content and the .site-footer. Under 900px the sidebar stacks on top. This page is built with it.

06 · Components

Buttons

Flat buttons with the containers' 2px corners and no shadows. .btn--primary (blue tint) is the one main action per view; .btn is everything else; .btn--danger is pink and turns maroon on hover; .link-btn--danger is red underlined text for resets; .btn--muted is a quiet grey (Settings); .btn--block fills the sidebar; .btn--icon is a square icon-only button (always with aria-label and title). .link-btn is for quiet inline actions like "all / clear".

Chosen words, chips, tags & logos

Words you've chosen are a .remove-list: "× word", no underline, removed on click. Keywords to avoid are red (darker on hover); search terms use .remove-list--find and are green. .chip and .tag are small square labels. .logo shows an outlet's image at one height (--logo-size): its wordmark from assets/logo where there's room, its square icon from assets/icon in small spots like the sidebar, then initials. The page trims each file's built-in padding so the marks line up, and a wordmark wider than --logo-fit × its height switches to the icon rather than shrinking.

Summary row, ruled grid & card

Above a feed, a .summary-row: Refresh first (.summary-bar__button, the same container style), then one .summary-bar line: "Displaying 87 articles (16 hidden)" on the left, "16 hidden" with a chevron on the right. It opens to list what was hidden, each with its reason and "show anyway"; with nothing hidden it doesn't open (.summary-bar--static). Below it, .ruled-grid gives 3 → 2 → 1 hairline columns of .cards, with the source logo in the card's top-left corner (no background behind it) and the time on the right. An article with no image shows the outlet's square icon, large, in the image space.

Settings & source lists

Settings is a .modal__panel--wide (860px, text a size larger than the sidebar) split into .panel-sections: content first (keywords, topics, bias, mood, sources), then Feed and Theme. Inputs with an action reuse .search. A list of records uses the FortifyAI agents-page pattern: a .toolbar (search, a .filter-pop of single-choice .chip-choices groups with a count .badge, and a grid/rows .view-toggle), then either a .data-table (rows) or a .source-grid of .source-cards. Credibility shows as a .cred-status (green check, yellow, red, grey); bias as a .bias-level (only High stands out, in red), with MBFC's label as a dashed .bias-badge on the detail page. A .selection-bar heads the list: select-all checkbox, "N of M in feed", Select all and Clear. Grid cards toggle on click and show selection with a thicker border and tint. Optional settings use a .switch. Clicking a record opens its .detail page in the same modal, with "← Back".
