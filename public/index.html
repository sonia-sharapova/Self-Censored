        Settings

        Configure your individual preferences for your news feed. Changes apply straight away and save as you go.

        Theme

        Redacted keywords

        • * extends a word to any ending or beginning: *ai hides both “AI” and “OpenAI”; kill* hides kill, killed and killings.
        • "quotes" match exactly, capitals included: "Trump" hides Trump but not a trump card.
        • A plain word matches that whole word in any case: war hides War and war, not software.

        Bias

        Filter news sources by their overall bias and fact checks. An outlet's leaning (left or right) is intentionally not shown, so you're more likely to form your own opinions.

        • Low least biased: grounded in what can be verified and measured
        • Medium leans left- or right-of-centre
        • High leans left or right

        Mood

        Tired of only hearing bad news? Set your desired tone to influence the articles displayed.

        Your data

        Self-Censored uses no cookies. It can remember your settings in this browser's local storage, but only if you allow it. Either way, you can export your configuration as a file and import it later (or on another device).

        Save settings on this deviceOff: your setup resets when you close the tab

        Defaults

        Your starting point. "Reset to default" puts these back. Set things up how you like them, then save them as your default.
