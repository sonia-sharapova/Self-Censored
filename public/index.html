        Sources

        Select which media outlets you would like to see. Favourite a news outlet with ☆ to have it easily accessible in the sidebar.

        All sources present: every outlet is already in your feed.

        Redacted keywords

        • * extends a word to any ending or beginning: *AI hides both “AI” and “OpenAI”; kill* hides kill, killed and killings.
        • "quotes" match exactly, capitals included: "Trump" hides Trump but not a trump card.
        • A plain word matches that whole word in any case: war hides War and war, not software.

        The Spectrum

        Bias

        Filter news sources by their overall bias and fact checks. An outlet's leaning (left or right) is intentionally not shown, so you're more likely to form your own opinions.

        • Low least biased: grounded in what can be verified and measured
        • Medium leans left- or right-of-centre
        • High leans left or right

        Mood
