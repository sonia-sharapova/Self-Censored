        Configure

        Tune what reaches you. Changes apply straight away and save as you go.

        Redacted keywords

        Articles that mention any ticked word are hidden (listed under "hidden" above the feed). Untick to stop redacting it. Use * for any ending, quotes for a phrase.

        Topics

        Toggle which topics reach your feed. An outlet shows up when it covers any selected topic.

        Sources

        The outlets in your feed. Click one for its details; ☆ star it to keep it in the sidebar; × takes it out.

        Bias

        How strongly an outlet leans, from Media Bias/Fact Check: low = least biased or pro-science, medium = left- or right-center, high = left or right. Pick one, or "all" (which also includes unrated outlets).

        Mood

        Each article's tone, guessed from its headline and summary words. Approximate. Pick one, or "all".

        Advanced settings

        How the feed is fetched and matched. These apply to every source and topic.

        Auto-refreshFetch new articles on a timer

        Appearance

        Choose how Self-Censored looks. Pick a theme, or sync with your system to switch between light and dark automatically.

        Defaults

        Your starting point. "Reset to default" (here and under Sources) puts these back. Set things up how you like them, then save them as your default.
